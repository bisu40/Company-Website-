## Overview

This repository contains the official corporate website of **Irium Labs (OPC) Private Limited**, an independent technology company focused on software engineering, cybersecurity, artificial intelligence, digital privacy, and advanced technology solutions.

The website serves as the company's official digital presence, providing corporate information, product information, legal disclosures, privacy policies, and official communication channels.

## Engineering Principles

The website is developed with an emphasis on:

- Security and privacy by design.
- Modern, accessible, and responsive interfaces.
- High-performance web architecture.
- Consistent corporate branding and visual identity.
- Advanced interactive graphics and visual experiences.
- Maintainable and scalable software engineering.
- Minimal reliance on unnecessary third-party services.
